02 — Thesis
Sheikh Zayed Road · SUNG —1975— Concept billboard · SS 2026

The right brand.
The right city.
The right window.

Dubai is the second athleisure capital of the world. ALO, Lululemon, Vuori, Gymshark and Giving Movement have all opened flagships within 36 months — into a UAE market valued at $13.26B (2024), compounding at 9.71% a year. What the city is short on: premium men's brands with a face you'd recognise.

SUNG1975 fits that shelf. Same design language. Same price tier ($75–$156). A founder whose personal channels reach 2.86 million people across Instagram and YouTube. What it lacks is meaningful penetration outside Korea and Japan.

And it sits in a lane the competitive set can't reach. ALO and Lululemon are studio-yoga. Vuori is California coastal. Gymshark is hard-gym DTC. Giving Movement is modest-fashion eco. SUNG1975 is the only founder-led, MMA-credibility lane on that shelf — and Dubai is the city that pays for personality, not for price.

We are proposing to close that gap — building the UAE & GCC arm of SUNG1975 under exclusive licence, fully funded and operated by us. No cash investment from your side. Asymmetric risk by design.

And no operating burden either. Akito's Dubai team handles everything end-to-end: UAE company formation, banking, trademark, visas, marketing, logistics, fulfillment. Akiyama-san signs zero forms.

05 — The reference case

Within four years: 13 stores across 5 countries (8 of them UAE), $15M Series A led by Knuru Capital + Turmeric Capital, and AED 20M+ raised for charity by Sept 2023 — implying 1.35M+ units sold.

Tee retail AED 249–299 ($67–81). Hoodie AED 499–599 ($135–163). Design: minimal, monochromatic, hero capsules with Arabic graphics — adjacent to SUNG1975's own aesthetic. Founder sold the business to existing investors May 2025 (publicly acknowledged May 2026). New CEO Rania Masri El Khatib explicitly pivoting back to MENA-first after a costly US push.

Giving Movement proved that a Dubai-based label can scale outward. We propose to use the same playbook in reverse — a Tokyo/Seoul label scaling inward through Dubai. Their cautionary tale is our strategic brief.
