The right brand.

Dubai is the second athleisure capital of the world. 04 — Why Dubai, why now

Dubai is quietly the world's
second athleisure capital.

Within 36 months every major premium athleisure brand has opened or committed to UAE retail — and the partner pattern has shifted. Digitally-native brands now bypass Alshaya (mass-market franchise) for Al Tayer or MAF (luxury-curated). Gymshark and Vuori both went Al Tayer. This is the new playbook for an athleisure brand entering Dubai.

The Dubai-born benchmark — Giving Movement — scaled from a single 2020 DTC site to 13 stores across 5 countries (8 in UAE) on a $15M Series A. Their founder sold to existing investors May 2025; the new CEO is explicitly pivoting back to MENA-first after a costly US push.

This city rewards brands with strong personality and design. It punishes brands that try to compete on price alone. SUNG1975 is positioned for the first lane, not the second.

05 — The audience gap

880K Instagram.
2M YouTube.
And the brand has 23,000.

The personal channel reaches 881,798. The brand's own channels — @sung1975_kr and @sung1975_japan combined — reach 23,172. That is a 38× gap between the founder's voice and the brand's voice. Closing that gap is the entire commercial opportunity of this venture.

And the personal channel's engagement rate is 21.19% — versus a mega-influencer median of roughly 1.5%. That is not a passive audience. It is one of the most active large personal audiences in the world, and it has barely been monetised through SUNG1975 yet.

Dubai is the most efficient single city in the world to plug a Western and MENA exposure layer onto that audience: English-speaking, sizeable Japanese and Korean diaspora, year-round European tourism, GCC HNW concentration, and the most aggressive premium-fitness-lifestyle market outside the US.

06 — The reference case

Giving Movement proved Dubai could be a global launchpad.

Founded 2020 in Dubai by Dominic Nowell-Barnes. Premium athleisure with a charitable mechanic ($4 donated per item). Within four years: 13 stores across 5 countries (8 of them UAE), $15M Series A led by Knuru Capital + Turmeric Capital, and AED 20M+ raised for charity by Sept 2023 — implying 1.35M+ units sold.

Tee retail AED 249–299 ($67–81). Hoodie AED 499–599 ($135–163). Design: minimal, monochromatic, hero capsules with Arabic graphics — adjacent to SUNG1975's own aesthetic. Founder sold the business to existing investors May 2025 (publicly acknowledged May 2026). New CEO Rania Masri El Khatib explicitly pivoting back to MENA-first after a costly US push.

Giving Movement proved that a Dubai-based label can scale outward. We propose to use the same playbook in reverse — a Tokyo/Seoul label scaling inward through Dubai. Their cautionary tale is our strategic brief.

11 — The UAE-only capsule

Arabic calligraphy. SUNG1975 minimalism.

Every successful premium label in this market drops a UAE-coded capsule. Nike commissioned Egyptian artist Mohamed Samir for Arabic geometric typography at its Dubai Mall flagship — anchor of the #weplaydxb campaign. Gymshark launched its first permanent international store (Dubai Mall, Jan 2025) with an Arabic-calligraphy capsule co-created with Lebanese-Canadian influencer Leana Deeb. Louis Vuitton × Nada Debs "LV Mirage" used mashrabiya patterns and dune-inspired motifs. Giving Movement runs its "Born in Dubai" graphic line continuously.

The pattern is proven and respected. We propose a SUNG1975 × UAE capsule each year. Two to four pieces. Single-colour Arabic typography on SUNG1975's existing silhouettes, untouched. Sold in airport, flagship, and online — never wholesale.

Cultural respect is non-negotiable. Calligraphy will be commissioned from a UAE-based Arabic typographer with credits, with usage reviewed by both sides before production. The mockups below are AI-generated concepts — not final designs.

14 — Parallel track · skincare

A second category. Same playbook, different shelf.

TWENTY FIFTY × SUNG 1975 EDITION Premium Nano Collagen Sheet × Mist

Your existing collaboration with TWENTY FIFTY — the Korean nanofiber collagen brand — is one of the most pitch-perfect products for this market we have ever seen. The GCC men's grooming market is $8.5B; the UAE alone runs $288M (2024) → $426.8M by 2030 at a 6.75% CAGR. UAE K-beauty separately grows from $31.6M (2023) → $51.1M (2032). And the men's K-beauty global segment compounds at 10.9% — the fastest of any K-beauty slice.

Korean skincare is already proven in the GCC through local-operator partnerships. Laneige opened its Dubai Mall flagship with AR mirrors May 2024. Beauty of Joseon, COSRX, Sulwhasoo are distributed through Watsons, Sephora ME, Boots, Bloomingdale's. JKOSMEC entered via Apparel Group + Carrefour October 2025. The model works.

Closest celebrity-skincare comparable: Le Domaine (Brad Pitt × Famille Perrin) launched late 2022 — cleansing emulsion $80, serum $385, cream $320. Distributed across France, UK, Italy, Germany, Switzerland, US; ME via The Rite Store. Deliberately understated "this is not a celebrity brand" framing — exactly the playbook for an Akiyama skincare line that needs to feel like a serious product, not a vanity project.
