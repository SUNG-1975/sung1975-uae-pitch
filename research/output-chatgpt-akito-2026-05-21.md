### Premium penetration

From the GymNation UAE/KSA survey (2026):
- **51%** of UAE respondents increased fitness spending in the last year
- **37%** of that increase went to fitness clothing or accessories
- **13%** went to premium memberships
- **53%** of UAE non-members cite price as the main barrier

Modelled premium-athleisure penetration: 20–30% of the broader athleisure market in the UAE, higher in Dubai/Abu Dhabi affluent districts and among tourists.

### Gym memberships and chain mix

- **GymNation**: 110,000+ members across UAE and KSA, 14 UAE + 6 KSA locations as of Jan 2025
- **>50%** of new GymNation members had no gym membership in the prior 12 months — market is expanding, not just reallocating
- Premium urban studios and chains fragmented; **Warehouse Gym** highly relevant for activation at intersection of serious training, combat-sports culture, and high-income membership

### Behavioural signals

- **79%** of UAE respondents work out at least twice a week
- **61%** use wearables
- **52%** interested in strength training
- Sample skew: 39% female, 61% male; 35% aged 25–34, 34% aged 35–44

## Part 7 — Revenue simulation

### TAM math

- **UAE resident population 20–50**: modelled from standard population structure
- **Fitness/combat/wellness engaged share**: 55–65% of that group (anchored by 79% "work out twice a week or more" signal, discounted for survey bias)
- **Premium-athleisure buyers within engaged group**: 20–35% (anchored by 37% apparel-spend signal + Dubai premium-retail density)
- **TAM result**: ~0.9M – 1.8M customers in UAE, working midpoint **1.3M**
- **GCC TAM**: 3.0M – 5.8M, midpoint **4.2M**

### ARPU assumptions

- Apparel ARPU **AED 1,050 – 2,200**, base case **AED 1,550** (3–4 pieces / yr)
- Skincare ARPU **AED 300 – 500**, base **AED 375**
- Exchange rate AED 3.67 = USD 1

### Simulation table (UAE only, 1.3M TAM)

| Scenario | Penetration | Customers | Apparel ARPU | Skincare ARPU | Total annual revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pessimistic | 1% | 13,000 | AED 1,050 / $286 | AED 300 / $82 | **AED 17.55M / $4.78M** |
| Base | 3% | 39,000 | AED 1,550 / $422 | AED 375 / $102 | **AED 75.53M / $20.56M** |
| **Target** | **5%** | **65,000** | **AED 1,800 / $490** | **AED 450 / $123** | **AED 146.25M / $39.84M** |
| Optimistic | 8% | 104,000 | AED 2,200 / $599 | AED 500 / $136 | **AED 280.80M / $76.49M** |

### Sensitivity

- 20% higher average ticket → revenue up 20%
- 2 percentage-point penetration increase (5% → 7%) → revenue up **40%**
- 20% lower ticket on target → AED 117M (from AED 146M)
- 2-point penetration miss → ~AED 88M

### Channel mix (steady state)

- **Own e-commerce: 40–45%**
- **Gym pop-ups: 15–20%**
- **Airport retail: 15–25%**
- **MMA event booths: 10–15%**

### First 12 months

- ~20–30% of steady-state target revenue
- Year 1 target case ≈ **AED 36.6M** (~$10M)

### Biggest data gaps

1. Exact UAE active gym membership totals + chain market shares
2. Verified brand-level UAE sales for Alo, Lululemon, Vuori, Giving Movement
3. Combat-sports media reach + event attendance by promotion
