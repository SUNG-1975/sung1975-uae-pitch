You are a market-research analyst. Produce a single deep-research report sizing the UAE and GCC wellness + MMA / combat-sports market for the launch of a premium athleisure brand, and run a revenue-capture simulation at the bottom.

# BRAND CONTEXT (so the simulation has a realistic anchor)

- Brand: SUNG1975 — Korean-Japanese premium athleisure, founded by MMA legend Choo Sung-hoon (秋山成勲, "Akiyama"). Existing Japan retail $75–$156 USD per piece.
- Proposed UAE pricing: tee AED 275 (~$75), hoodie AED 500–600, pants AED 470, premium hoodie AED 575.
- Adjacent product: TWENTY FIFTY × SUNG 1975 skincare collab (Premium Nano Collagen Sheet × Mist), $80–$120 retail.
- Target customer: 25–45-year-old residents and tourists in the UAE who engage in fitness (gym, MMA, BJJ, CrossFit, running), buy premium athleisure ($60+ per piece), and have visible disposable income.
- Channels: own .ae e-commerce + gym pop-ups (Warehouse Gym chain) + DXB Duty Free airport retail + MMA event booth presence (Dubai Muscle Show, AJP, UFC Abu Dhabi, UAE Warriors).

# DELIVERABLE STRUCTURE

Produce the report in this exact order, with tables wherever practical and a source URL + publication date next to every figure.
