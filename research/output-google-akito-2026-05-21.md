## Executive summary

The UAE wellness and athleisure market is undergoing a rapid maturation phase:
- UAE **wellness tourism sector** valued at **USD 15.1B in 2025**
- UAE **athleisure market** estimated at **USD 18.67B in 2025**
- 59% year-on-year increase in gym engagement and health aspirations among UAE and KSA residents

Based on conservative 3% market penetration of the estimated **1.8M addressable premium-wellness consumers**, SUNG1975 base-case annual revenue: **AED 81M (~$22M USD)**. Target case (5%): **AED 135M / ~$36.7M**.

Confidence: high, given convergence of post-2025 tourism records and established "premium" spending behaviors in Dubai and Riyadh.

## Part 3 — UAE + GCC MMA / combat-sports market

Combat sports represent the highest-intent segment for SUNG1975's branding.

## Part 5 — Tourist / airport channel

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| DXB passenger volume 2025 | **95.2M** |

| Best-performing duty-free categories | Premium skincare / small giftable goods |

**Channel strategy implication**: duty-free in DXB performs best with giftable items due to weight/space constraints in carry-on luggage. Skincare collab fits airport better than apparel.

## Part 7 — Revenue capture simulation

### TAM definition
**1.8M** active, high-income fitness residents (age 25–45) in the UAE/KSA combined.

### Assumptions
- **Apparel ARPU**: AED 1,100 / yr (average 2 items)

### Sensitivity
- **Pricing (+20%)**: a move to AED 330/tee could contract customer volume by 10% but maintain revenue parity via margin expansion
- **Penetration (±2pp)**: revenue swings linearly by **±AED 54M** at the target customer base

3. **Skincare purchase intent**: lacks specific conversion benchmarks for fitness-centric skincare in the GCC (requires A/B testing or pilot survey)
