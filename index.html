2.3The audience gap — and why Dubai closes it.

Akiyama's personal channels reach roughly 2.86M people across IG and YouTube. The brand reaches 23,000.

Akiyama's audience skews overwhelmingly Korean and Japanese with a long tail through East and Southeast Asia. The Western market — Europe, North America, MENA — has not been served. And the brand has not yet inherited the founder's reach.

Dubai is the most efficient single city in the world to fix both at once. It is English-speaking. It has sizeable Japanese and Korean diaspora communities. It hosts European tourism year-round in volume. It is one of the largest GCC HNW concentrations. It is the airport hub that processed 92.3 million passengers in 2024 — the world's busiest international airport for the tenth consecutive year.

Get traction in Dubai and Riyadh, and you simultaneously open three exposure layers: GCC residents, European tourists, and the press cycle that follows them home.

One more audience truth worth knowing.

Western audience growth post-Physical: 100 (Netflix, Jan 2023) is real but not yet quantified in any public source. Geographic and demographic breakdowns require paid influencer analytics (HypeAuditor, Modash) — typically $50–$100 for a one-time pull. We recommend running that before the term sheet so the Phase 1 marketing plan is built on real numbers, not directional assumptions.
