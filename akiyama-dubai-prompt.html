I am creating a hero portrait for a pitch deck. The brand is SUNG1975 — the Korean-Japanese athleisure label of MMA legend Choo Sung-hoon (秋山成勲 / Yoshihiro Akiyama), the silver-fox star of Netflix's *Physical: 100*. The attached image is him in his own SS 2026 campaign, wearing a grey SUNG1975 varsity tee against an urban backdrop.

Generate a photorealistic editorial composite portrait of the SAME man, wearing the SAME (or visually identical) grey SUNG1975 varsity tee, standing in the foreground with the Dubai skyline behind him at golden hour.

COMPOSITION
- Akiyama: 3/4 portrait, chest-up, centred horizontally, occupying the lower 60% of the frame
- His face and expression: keep the calm, intense look from the reference photo. Do not soften him into a smile.
- Background: Dubai skyline at golden hour. Burj Khalifa clearly visible off-centre (left or right of his head, never directly behind it). Marina-area towers in middle distance, clean blue-to-gold sky gradient.
- Negative space: deliberate, around him — the skyline should breathe, not crowd the subject
- Aspect: generate both 4:5 portrait AND 16:9 landscape if the tool supports it; otherwise default to 4:5

LIGHTING
- Time of day: ~17:30 Dubai, golden hour
- Direction: warm key light from camera-left, gentle fill from the sky
- Slight atmospheric haze in the distant skyline (Dubai dust at sunset)
- His face cleanly lit; skin tone natural, no over-tanning or AI smoothing

STYLE
- Editorial campaign photography quality — reference points: Vuori, Lululemon, Brunello Cucinelli sportswear campaign aesthetic
- Brand colour palette feel: cream / olive / warm-charcoal · the grade should feel warm but restrained, never saturated
- Photorealistic, no AI-art surrealism, no over-rendered skin
- No on-screen text, no SUNG1975 wordmark added, no brand graphics inserted

FACTUAL & CULTURAL
- The skyline must be unambiguously Dubai — Burj Khalifa silhouette is the anchor
- No religious imagery in frame (no mosques, no crescents)
- No other people in frame
- No vehicles, no distracting foreground objects
- No watermark, no border

OUTPUT
- High resolution, suitable for use as a full-bleed deck hero image
- Sharp focus on Akiyama; mild atmospheric depth-of-field on the skyline
- Clean, deck-grade, no visible AI artefacts (no extra fingers, no warped buildings, no melted neon)

VARIANTS TO RUN (do each as a separate generation, then I'll pick):
1. Default — Akiyama facing camera, calm intense look (the hero shot)
2. Quarter-turn — Akiyama looking out toward the skyline (3/4 back view, contemplative)
3. Tighter crop, more atmospheric — Akiyama centre, slight dust haze, more cinematic mood
4. Lifestyle energy — same composition but with a half-smile and slightly more open posture
5. Late-evening grade — same shot, blue-hour instead of golden hour, cooler palette
