I am creating a mockup billboard advertisement for the launch of SUNG1975 — a Korean athleisure brand founded by Choo Sung-hoon / Yoshihiro Akiyama, the MMA legend — entering the UAE market.

I am attaching TWO reference images:
1. A reference photo of a Samsung billboard on Sheikh Zayed Road near the Dubai Frame in Dubai — use it ONLY for the composition, location, perspective, billboard placement, road framing, and overall photographic mood. Do NOT carry over any Samsung branding, blue colours, models, or copy.
2. A portrait of Akiyama (the man in the grey SUNG1975 varsity tee). He must be the hero subject of the billboard.

Generate a photorealistic mockup of an outdoor billboard in Dubai with this exact composition:

LOCATION & PERSPECTIVE
- Setting: Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai, near the Dubai Frame (the iconic gold rectangular monument). The Dubai Frame should be visible to one side of the billboard in the background skyline.
- Time of day: clear daylight, slightly warm afternoon cast (around 16:00)
- Camera position: shot from across the road at slight elevation, similar framing to the reference image
- Foreground: 6-lane Sheikh Zayed Road with realistic Dubai traffic — Toyota Land Cruisers, Mercedes G-Wagons, Range Rovers, dark sedans. Manicured palm trees and red flower beds lining the road.
- Background: Dubai skyline behind the billboard — Burj-area towers, glass facades, clean blue sky

BILLBOARD CONTENT
- Format: wide horizontal billboard, roughly 3:1 aspect ratio, modern minimalist outdoor structure
- Background colour of the billboard panel: clean off-white / warm cream (#F0EBE2), NOT bright blue
- Top-left of billboard: brand wordmark "SUNG —1975—" in clean modern Latin sans-serif, dark charcoal ink (#1A1815), medium weight
- Top-right of billboard: Arabic transliteration "سونغ ١٩٧٥" in modern minimalist Arabic typography (geometric Kufi / Reem Kufi / Cairo style — NOT Thuluth, NOT Diwani, NOT decorative), same dark charcoal colour
- Center of billboard: the attached Akiyama portrait. Place him at full billboard height, occupying roughly 35-40% of the billboard width, slightly left of centre. Keep his pose and expression from the reference photo. Cleanly composited against the cream background — no harsh masking, soft natural shadow at his feet.
- Right of centre, large headline: "Continuous challenge." in clean Fraunces serif (or modern serif), italic only on "challenge", dark charcoal ink
- Below the English headline, smaller: the Arabic line "تحدّي مستمر" in clean editorial Arabic typeface, same dark colour
- Bottom-right of billboard, very small: "sung1975w.com" in mono font, dark charcoal
- Bottom-left of billboard, very small: "الآن في دبي" (Arabic: "Now in Dubai") in mono, same dark colour

STYLE & CULTURAL RESPECT
- Photorealistic, deck-grade, pitch-quality output
- Cinematic colour grade with slight warm cast
- Sharp focus on the billboard, mild depth-of-field softening on cars in foreground
- Do NOT include religious imagery, mosques, crescents, or any religious symbology
- Do NOT include any other brand logos (no Samsung, Galaxy, etc.)
- The result should feel like a real Dubai out-of-home placement — commercial, modern, premium, design-forward

OUTPUT
- 16:9 cinematic aspect ratio
- High resolution suitable for a pitch deck slide
- Sharp, clean, no visible AI artefacts
